BUILT FOR CLARITY

Signals, not
unverifiable promises.

Funding announcements are a starting point for research, not a complete company database.

01 / DISCOVER

Broad coverage, checked hourly

Publisher feeds and Google News searches discover startup funding, seed, Series A/B, growth rounds, India coverage and press releases. News-search links are labeled by publisher. Indexing and publication delays mean this is not an instant or complete feed.

02 / QUALIFY

Reposts are not new rounds

Reviewed announcement dates and earliest matching indexed reports are separate from article publication and discovery. Historical searches are bounded and may miss earlier reports; choose Reviewed dates only for checked announcements. Company location needs explicit headline or reviewed evidence; unknowns stay Unknown. Amount and stage are headline-extracted, not independently verified.

03 / CONNECT

Contacts stay private

This public site never displays founder email addresses. Optional Hunter enrichment requires a secret API key and reviewed company domains; results are private workflow artifacts, not automatic outreach.
